## Board (rev F K21 / "Top cap")
- **28 × 6 × 0.8 mm, 2 layers.** x = 0 at the outer (port) end. Every part sits on the **back** (inner side); the front faces the 1.0 mm gasket and carries only the GND pour and short crossings.
- **Port:** NPTH Ø0.8 at (2.0, 3.0), under MK1's sound port (rev F port at x_rel ±35.5, strip outer end ±37.5).
- **Heat-stake holes:** NPTH Ø2.0 at (7.5, 3.0) and (26.0, 3.0) for the Ø1.8 pins (x_rel ±30.0 / ±11.5); Ø3.6 back keep-outs (no copper, no parts) for the staked heads.
- One design for both sides: the left strip is the right one turned 180° in the cap plane (port end outward, gasket side up).
- Rules: tracks 0.2 (0.25–0.3 for supply and ground), clearance 0.15, vias 0.45/0.2, copper to edge 0.3 (JLC capabilities, checked 2026-11-07).
