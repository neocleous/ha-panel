Main board · step 1 of 23

this step already on the board not yet

Test-fit the 5 V power module

Do
  1. Push the 5 V power module (PS1) into its 13 holes, pin 1 at the end marked “+Vo”. Don't solder.
  2. Hold it upright and look straight down.
Note

Pass: the body stays inside its printed rectangle, clear of the outlines of the two large round capacitors and the coil. Fail: stop; don't build this board.

Check the bare board for shorts

Do
  1. Meter on ohms, black probe on the ground test pad (TP7).
  2. Red probe in turn on 20 V (TP2), 5 V (TP9), 3.3 V (TP16) and the right-hand pad of the amplifier's fuse (F5 pad 2).
Note

Pass: each reads open. Fail: stop; the board is faulty.

Paste the board

Do
  1. Line the main board's stencil up on the pads; tape it down along one edge.
  2. Paste with one firm pass of the squeegee. Lift the stencil straight up.
Note

Paste on every surface-mount pad, the empty places too; none on the test pads or the through-holes.

Tip

Smeared or thin under the amplifier: wipe the board with IPA on a lint-free wipe, let it dry, paste again.

Place the three chips

Do
  1. Place the amplifier chip (U100) and the 20 V protection chip (U1): the dot at the red ring.
  2. Place the 3.3 V regulator (U2): three legs on the three-pad side; it fits one way.
Tip

Set each chip down square in one go; nudge it on its pads, don't drag it through the paste.

Place the diodes

Do
  1. Place the 20 V surge diode (D1) and the 5 V surge diode (D2), each straight from its own tape: they look alike.
  2. Turn each so the band is at the red ring.

Place the small resistors (0603), one value at a time

Do
  1. 412 kΩ: R1
  2. 9.09 kΩ: R2
  3. 22.1 kΩ: R3
  4. 5.49 kΩ: R4
  5. 10 kΩ: R5 R10 R11
  6. 100 kΩ: R6 R13 R17 R18 R100 R102 R103 R104 R105
  7. 150 kΩ: R101
  8. 4.7 kΩ: R19
  9. 0 Ω: R7 R14 R15 R16 R26
  10. Leave empty: R20 R21 R22 R23 R24 R25 R27
Tip

Straight from its tape; close the tape before the next.

Place the small capacitors (0603), one value at a time

Do
  1. 100 nF: C2 C4 C9 C100 C121
  2. 47 nF: C3
  3. 0.22 µF: C104 C108 C111 C115
  4. 1 µF, 50 V: C103
  5. 1 µF, 25 V: C105 C112 C118 C119 C120
  6. 2.2 µF: C106 C107 C113 C114
Tip

Capacitors carry no marking: straight from the tape, close it before the next.

Place the 0805 parts, one value at a time

Do
  1. 2.2 Ω: R12
  2. 1 µF: C1 C10
  3. 0.68 µF: C109 C110 C116 C117
  4. 4.7 µF: C12 C101 C102 C122 C123
  5. Leave empty: R8 R9

Place the fuses and 1206 capacitors

Do
  1. Place 10 µF (1206): C8 C11
  2. Place the resettable fuses: 0.2 A (F4), the small one; 2.6 A (F5), the large one.
  3. Place the fuses: 5 A (F1), 10 A (F2), 1 A (F3).
Note

F1, F2 and F3 look alike: place each straight from its own tape.

Place the coils

Do
  1. Place 10 µH: L100 L101 L102 L103
  2. Place the large 15 µH coil (L1) last.

Check before heating

Do
  1. Compare the board with the drawing: every pasted place has its part, the nine empty places stay empty.
  2. Check each part sits square on its pads; chips and diodes match the red rings.
Tip

A photo now makes the inspection after the hotplate quicker.

Reflow on the hotplate

Do
  1. Lay the board on the hotplate, door side up.
  2. Run the paste's profile: up to 150 °C, on through 183 °C, peak 235 °C, about 240 s in all.
  3. Let it cool where it lies until the solder has set.
Note

One pass only.

Inspect and touch up

Do
  1. Under magnification, check every pin of the amplifier and protection chips (U100, U1) for bridges and lifted pins.
  2. Check every other joint.
Tip

A bridge: flux on it, then a clean iron tip along the pins, or wick. Leave the flux residue.

Check the supplies for shorts

Do
  1. Meter on ohms, black probe on the ground test pad (TP7). Red probe in turn on 20 V in (TP1), 20 V (TP2), 5 V (TP9), 3.3 V (TP16) and the right-hand pad of the amplifier's fuse (F5 pad 2).
Note

Pass: each reads open, or climbs toward open as the capacitors charge. Fail: a steady few ohms or less: find the short before going on.

Fit the wire link

Do
  1. Fit the wire link (LK1): 1.0 mm tinned copper wire, in from the door side, soldered on the back. Every through-hole part goes in the same way.
Tip

Solder one end, check it lies flat, then the other.

Fit the Pi power socket

Do
  1. Fit the Pi power socket (J2) flat on the board.
  2. Solder all 16 contacts and the 4 shell stakes.
Tip

Fine tip; fill each hole: the pins barely come through.

Fit the plug sockets

Do
  1. Fit the 2-way sockets: 20 V (J1) in the middle, left speaker (J8), right speaker (J9).
  2. Fit the 4-way sockets: radar (J5), touch (J6).
  3. Each flat on the board, its outline on the printed one.
  4. With a fine marker, beside each socket: 20 V (J1) a red dot; radar (J5) R; touch (J6) T.
Tip

Solder one pin, check the socket sits flat, re-melt if not, then the rest.

Fit the sensor headers

Do
  1. Before fitting, mark beside each header's square pad (pin 1): BME680 (J10) B; light sensor (J11) L.
  2. Fit the two 1 × 4 pin headers, short pins through the board.

Fit the small capacitor

Do
  1. Fit 22 µF (C7): the long (+) lead in the hole at the red ring. The stripe on the can marks −.

Fit the large capacitors

Do
  1. Fit 120 µF (C5, C6): the long (+) lead in the hole at the red ring, seated on the board. The stripe marks −.

Fit the power module

Do
  1. Fit the 5 V power module (PS1): pin 1 at the red ring, the end marked “+Vo”; upright and seated.
  2. Solder the two end pins, check it is upright, then the rest.

Trim the leads

Do
  1. Cut the leads of the three round capacitors and the wire link (C5, C6, C7, LK1) as short as flush cutters go, 1.5 mm at most.
Note

The row of pads at the top left (J7) stays empty.

Check the supplies again

Do
  1. Meter on ohms, black probe on the ground test pad (TP7). Red probe in turn on 20 V in (TP1), 20 V (TP2), 5 V (TP9), 3.3 V (TP16) and the right-hand pad of the amplifier's fuse (F5 pad 2).
Note

Pass: each reads open, or climbs toward open as the capacitors charge. Fail: a steady few ohms or less: find the short before going on.

Check the bare strips

Do
  1. Hold each strip back up, sound-hole end to the right: pad 1 is the top pad of the five.
  2. Hold each strip to the light.
  3. Meter on ohms across pads 1 and 2.
Note

Pass: light through the sound hole; pads 1–2 open. Every step from here is for both strips.

Tape the sound hole

Do
  1. Put Kapton tape over the sound hole on the plain face, the one that meets the gasket.
Note

Keep flux, paste, water, alcohol and compressed air away from the hole.

Set the strip in a jig

Do
  1. Lay the strip face-down in a pocket its size, or ringed by spare strips each on a layer of Kapton, its back level with them.

Paste the back

Do
  1. Line up the strip's openings on the second stencil; tape over the touch board's.
  2. Paste with one pass of the squeegee; lift the stencil straight up.
Note

No paste in the sound hole or inside the microphone's ring-shaped pad. Any there: wipe the strip clean and paste again.

Place the logic chip

Do
  1. Place the logic chip (U1): three legs on the three-pad side; it fits one way.

Place the diodes

Do
  1. Place the three protection diodes (D1, D2, D3): the end with the dot or notch at the red ring, the closed end of the printed bracket.

Place the resistors and capacitors

Do
  1. Place 100 Ω: R1
  2. Place 100 kΩ: R2
  3. Place 33 Ω: R4
  4. Place 0 Ω: R5
  5. Place 10 µF: C1
  6. Place 100 nF: C2 C3

Place the microphone

Do
  1. Place the microphone (MK1) last: lid up, its sound port over the hole. Hold it by the lid only.

Reflow on the hotplate

Do
  1. Lay the strip face-down on the hotplate, as it lay for pasting.
  2. Run the same profile as the main board: peak 235 °C, about 240 s.
  3. Let it cool where it lies.
Note

One pass. Rework only if you must: the microphone takes only two more heatings.

Untape and check

Do
  1. Once cool, peel off the Kapton; no washing.
  2. Meter on diode test: red on pad 2, black in turn on pads 1, 3 and 5; then reversed.
  3. Meter on ohms across pads 1 and 2.
  4. Look: the 0 Ω link (R5) is fitted.
Note

Pass: each conducts one way and is open the other (pad 1 climbs to open); pads 1–2 not 0 Ω.
