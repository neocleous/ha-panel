HA Touch Panel · electronics

HA panel electronics

A USB-C charger feeds 20 V through the inlet board to the main board. The main board protects it, makes 5 V for the Pi and the radar and 3.3 V for the sensors, and drives two speakers from a TAS5756M. The Pi talks to the amplifier, the touch board and both sensors on one I²C bus, to the radar over UART0, and sends audio over I²S. The ReSpeaker Lite takes the two microphone strips and reaches the Pi over USB.

  • Supply 20 V, USB-C PD
  • Rails 5 V (NID100-5) · 3.3 V (TPS70933)
  • I²C1 GPIO2 / GPIO3: 1b, 48, 4c, 76/77
  • UART0 GPIO14 / 15, 256000 8N1
  • I²S GPIO18–21
  • Mute GPIO22
  • Boards main rev 3.3 · touch rev E · mic strip rev 3 × 2
20 V goes on first, on the middle socket

J9, J1 and J8 are three identical 2-way sockets in one row. J1 is the middle one. A 20 V plug on a speaker socket can destroy the board. Mark the 20 V plug and the board beside J1.

Measure the power module before soldering it

From PS1's row of pins to its component side: about 5.0 mm, carry on. About 8.3 mm, stop: the board is wrong for it. Anything else, stop.

Pins 2 and 4 on the Pi are both 5 V

The display's power lead goes on pins 2-4-6, GND on pin 6. Check the row before pressing the housing home.

Before you solder

  1. Power module PS1 first: measure from its row of pins to its component side. About 5.0 mm: carry on. About 8.3 mm: stop, the board is wrong for it. Anything else: stop and look again.
  2. Open moisture-sensitive bags on the day. U100 TAS5756M, level 3: within 168 h. U1 TPS16630, level 2: 1 year. F4 and F5, level 2a: 4 weeks. On each mic strip D1–D3 ESD321, level 3: 168 h. All at 30 °C / 60 % or less; a part open longer is baked per J-STD-033 first.
  3. Bare main board, meter on ohms: TP2, F5 pad 2, TP9, TP16 each read open to TP7 (ground). Bare touch board: VCC to GND on the HOST pads open. Bare strip: J1 pads 1–2 open, and the Ø0.8 port hole open against the light.
  4. Paste: Sn63/Pb37 no-clean (Chip Quik SMD291AXT4), check its date. Main board through its own stencil, outline to outline. Touch board and strips through their shared stencil, lying front-down; its patterns are already mirrored. The strip needs a flush jig; Kapton over its port hole on the front.
  5. Leave empty: R8 R9 R20 R21 R22 R23 R24 R25 R27 and the wire pads J7. Their pads still get paste.
  6. Polarity: D1 and D2 band (cathode) to pad 1. U1, U2, U100 pin 1 at the red dot on the drawing. C5, C6, C7: + lead in pad 1, the stripe on the can is −. F1, F2 and F3 share one body (5 A, 10 A, 1 A): read the marking. On the strips: D1–D3 pin 1 is GND; U1 pin 1 at the chamfer; MK1 last, lid up.
  7. Hotplate: one pass per board, parts side up (touch board and strips front-down on a clean sheet). Follow the paste's profile, about 240 s, peak 235 °C. No washing.
  8. Through-hole parts by iron, low to high: LK1 (1.0 mm tinned copper wire) · J2 (all 16 contacts and the 4 shell stakes) · J1, J8, J9, J5, J6 · J10, J11 · C7 · C5, C6 · PS1, pin 1 at the end marked "+Vo". Cut the leads of C5, C6, C7 and LK1 to 1.5 mm or less behind the board.
  9. Pi signal wires on J3 and J4: cut the contact off the board end, push the wire in from the back face (marked "PI 1-15" and "PI 35-40") and solder it on the door side. Seen from the back the board is mirrored.

Stage 3 · configuration and buses

  1. Apply the Pi configuration (above, with the header), then plug the BME680 (J10), VEML6030 (J11), touch (J6) and radar (J5).
  2. Switch the 20 V off and on with everything plugged: TP9 rises to 4.90–5.10 V once, without the module restarting, and the Pi boots.
  3. sudo i2cdetect -y 1 shows 1b touch, 48 VEML6030, UU at 4c amplifier (held by its driver), and the BME680 at 76 or 77.
  4. Radar: read TP12 with the radar running and the Pi under full load; no invalid frame at 256000 8N1 on /dev/ttyAMA0.
  5. Microphones: with both strips on the ReSpeaker, arecord -l lists it; then the audio bench test.
