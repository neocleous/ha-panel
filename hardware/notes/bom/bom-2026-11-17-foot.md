## 5. Notes for the parts list
1. **C101, C102, C122, C123: why 4.7 µF where TI's table says 22 µF.** TI's datasheet (SLAS988B, Table 22) names 22 µF X5R 0805 rated above 1.45 × the supply and gives no minimum capacitance. At the board's 20 V a 22 µF 35 V 0805 keeps about 1.9 µF (TDK's curve for the part on TI's own evaluation board: 2.32 µF at 16 V, 1.46 µF at 25 V). The TDK 4.7 µF 50 V X7R keeps the same (2.37 µF and 1.41 µF). Read on product.tdk.com on 2026-11-17; record in `../main-board/rev3.2/parts-2026-11-17/README.md`.
2. **C12: why a 50 V part on the 3.3 V output.** It is the same TDK part, so that the boards have one 0805 part that reads 4.7 µF (D202). At 3.3 V it keeps about 4.6 µF (TDK's curve: 4.62 µF at 3.15 V, 4.50 µF at 4 V; read 2026-11-17); the regulator needs 2.0 µF (`../main-board/NOTES.md` §3).
3. **Three values on the schematics are higher than the chosen part's rating, by the decisions of 2026-11-08** (`digikey-bom.md`, column "Note"):
   - C8, C11 "10u 25V": KEMET C1206C106K4RACTU is a 16 V part (5 V rails);
   - C106, C107, C113, C114 "2.2u 25V": Yageo CC0603KRX7R7BB225 is a 16 V part (audio coupling at 3.3 V or less);
   - strip C1 and touch C2 "10u 10V": Taiyo Yuden EMK107BBJ106MA-T is a 16 V part (higher than asked).
4. **U2** is the TPS70933QDBVRQ1, the substitute chosen on 2026-11-08 for the TPS70933DBVR (same pinout).
5. **Places left empty:** R8, R9 (0805; a value only if the bench asks for the 5.1 V option) and R20–R25, R27 (0 Ω option links). The 0 Ω line holds 25 pieces for 7 fitted and these 7 places.
6. **The 100 Ω line** also serves the strip's R4 option (100 Ω instead of 33 Ω): 2 more of its 10.
7. **Not parts:** 19 test pads, the wire pads J3, J4 and J7, and the four mounting holes are copper and holes in the main board. The mic strip's five wire pads and the touch board's HOST pads and electrodes likewise.
8. **The 10 DigiKey lines that are not board parts** are the parts list's (`claude/parts-list-2026-11-16.md` §2 and §8, in the project): the leads' stock, the inlet board, the ReSpeaker, two bench lines and the feet.
9. **Prices and stock** in `digikey-bom.md` are those read on 2026-11-16, and on 2026-11-17 for the capacitor line. None was looked up by this script.

## 6. How this list is made
`python3 hardware/notes/bom/boards_bom.py hardware 2026-11-17`
- It reads the three schematics through kicad-cli 9.0.9. Each symbol carries the maker, the maker's part number and the DigiKey number.
- It reads the need and the quantity of each DigiKey line from `digikey-bom.md` and checks them against `digikey-bom.csv`, the basket's quick-add list.
- It stops if a footprint has no package entry, if the two DigiKey files disagree, or if a board part's need or spares no longer fit its line.
- Run log: `bom/boards_bom-2026-11-17-run.txt`.
