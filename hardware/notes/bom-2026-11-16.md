## 5. Notes for the parts list
1. **Three values on the schematics are higher than the chosen part's rating, by the decisions of 2026-11-08** (`digikey-bom.md`, column "Note"):
   - C8, C11 "10u 25V": KEMET C1206C106K4RACTU is a 16 V part (5 V rails);
   - C106, C107, C113, C114 "2.2u 25V": Yageo CC0603KRX7R7BB225 is a 16 V part (audio coupling at 3.3 V or less);
   - strip C1 and touch C2 "10u 10V": Taiyo Yuden EMK107BBJ106MA-T is a 16 V part (higher than asked).
2. **U2** is the TPS70933QDBVRQ1, the substitute chosen on 2026-11-08 for the TPS70933DBVR (same pinout).
3. **Places left empty:** R8, R9 (0805; a value only if the bench asks for the 5.1 V option) and R20–R25, R27 (0 Ω option links). The 0 Ω line holds 25 pieces for 7 fitted and these 7 places.
4. **The 100 Ω line** also serves the strip's R4 option (100 Ω instead of 33 Ω): 2 more of its 10.
5. **Not parts:** 19 test pads, the wire pads J3, J4 and J7, and the four mounting holes are copper and holes in the main board. The mic strip's five wire pads and the touch board's HOST pads and electrodes likewise.
6. **The 10 DigiKey lines that are not board parts** were not re-derived here. What the boards say about them: the XH sockets are as before (3 × 2-pin: J1, J8, J9; 2 × 4-pin: J5, J6); the Pi leads are 13 as before; the two sensor leads have 4 wires each as before. Whether the two SC1132 ribbons stay is the parts list's call (D170).
7. **Prices and stock were not looked up.** The figures of 2026-11-08 in `digikey-bom.md` are 8 days old.

## 6. How this list is made
`python3 hardware/notes/bom/boards_bom.py hardware 2026-11-16`
- It reads the three schematics through kicad-cli 9.0.9. Each symbol carries the maker, the maker's part number and the DigiKey number.
- It reads the need and the quantity of each DigiKey line from `digikey-bom.md` and checks them against `digikey-bom.csv`, the basket's quick-add list.
- It stops if a footprint has no package entry, or if the two DigiKey files disagree.
- Run log: `bom/boards_bom-2026-11-16-run.txt`.
